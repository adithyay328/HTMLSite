As of now, we only have 1 page: the home page, containing some details about me. It's a good start, and still will be the homepage for the site. But, what content pages do we need?

Let's keep it simple for now:
- Posts contains a list of decently long form posts, which contain motivation for why something is important, in depth explanations, and form a narrative on how to solve a problem
- How to pages. These are supposed to be shorter, and straight to the point, with no need for explanations. Meant to allow me to publish quick formulas, code, and method without having to write a full post on it.

Later, we can re-structure this. For now, this is good enough. All core nav is still on the homepage, to keep the page lightweight.
